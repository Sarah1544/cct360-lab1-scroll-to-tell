One Day on the Trail A story told by scrolling
6:10 am

The start

The trailhead is empty. Frost sits on the sign and the air smells of pine. Every step forward moves the mountains a little, and the sun climbs with you.

Above the treeline

The clouds move faster than you do.

12:40 pm

The ridge

Lunch on a flat rock. Below, the valley you crossed this morning looks like a map of itself. You realise the hard part is already behind you.

Golden hour

The light turns gold and every shadow gets longer.

7:55 pm

The descent

Knees complain. Headlamp on. The path you could not see this morning is now the only thing you can see, one small circle of light at a time.

Back at the trailhead. Same sign, same frost, different you. Back to the top
