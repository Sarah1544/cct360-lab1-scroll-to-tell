One Day in New York Sunrise to skyline, told by scrolling
6:10 AM

Brooklyn Bridge

We got up before the city did. Halfway across the bridge the sun came up over the East River, the cables turned gold, and for a few minutes it felt like we had Manhattan to ourselves.

Bubby's

Pancakes, strawberries and a tiny jug of maple syrup.

9:30 AM

Breakfast in Tribeca

The reward for the early start. Two stacks of pancakes, whipped cream, and a sweater that says exactly where we are. We took our time.

On the water

The ferry swings out past Liberty Island.

2:15 PM

The ferry

Wind, spray, and everyone crowding the rail on one side. The Statue of Liberty looks small from the bridge and enormous from the deck. From here you can see the whole skyline you are about to climb.
